# Questions for the tax team

The rule pack ships as DRAFT and every return pack is watermarked until these are settled and the
pack is signed off. Answers usually change configuration in the rule pack, not code.

| # | Question | What the build does until answered |
|---|----------|------------------------------------|
| 1 | Does a deadline on a weekend or public holiday move to the next working day? 28 Feb 2027 is a Sunday. | Due date stays on the 28th day after period end (the safe side). A setting in the rule pack turns on weekend rollover; public holidays are not modelled. |
| 2 | Which VAT201 box does the seller of domestic reverse charge goods use? I found no source for this. | Seller-side DRC lines go in no box and raise a warning. |
| 3 | Has the DRC scope grown beyond gold, diamonds, hydrocarbons and electronic devices? Secondary sources mention other precious metals and stones, and scrap metal. | Wider terms are detected and flagged for review. Treatment changes only when a tax code is mapped to DRC. |
| 4 | Should a return carry the e-invoice value or the ERP value when they differ within the AED 1 rounding tolerance? | The ledger uses ERP values. R03 limits the difference per invoice, and the reconciliation certificate shows the total variance against Corner 5. |
| 5 | Which FX rate applies on a day with no published Central Bank rate? | The line is held and a blocking exception is raised. Nothing falls back silently. |
| 6 | Is the current penalty regime Cabinet Decision 129 of 2025, effective 14 April 2026? Please check the figures in the rule pack against the Arabic text. They come from an Andersen comparison and a Legal 500 article based on a non-official translation: late filing AED 1,000 then 2,000 within 24 months, late payment 14% a year computed monthly, incorrect return AED 500, disclosure 1% a month, undisclosed before audit 15% plus 1% a month, cap 200% of the tax. | The pack uses these, and every estimate is labelled as one. Before 14 April 2026 there is no schedule, so the calculator says it cannot estimate. |
| 7 | Are the designated zone rules in the pack right, for goods within zones and goods sold to the mainland? | Goods within zones are outside scope when unmapped. Goods from a zone to the mainland are flagged. A mapped tax code always stands. |
| 8 | For partial unpaid supplier invoices, is the 6-month reversal pro rata to the unpaid part? | Reversal is pro rata. |
| 9 | Do the 1 Jan 2026 amendments change any calculation in the MVP? | Treated as no change in calculation: self-invoicing affects documents, the refund time limit affects COR-04, and input denial for known evasion is a manual judgement. |
| 10 | Please verify the five golden returns in `fixtures/golden`. | I computed the expected values, so they are not independent evidence. |
| 11 | A correction to a box with no adjustment column (such as 3, 6, 7 or 10): where should it go in the next return? | It posts to the first output or input box that has an adjustment column, with a description naming the original box. |
| 12 | When is reverse charge VAT accounted for on imported services and DRC purchases, when the supply date and invoice date fall in different periods? | A purchase follows its invoice date unless the file gives an explicit `tax_point_date`. |
| 13 | Are the blocked input and designated zone text patterns in the rule pack right? They are my starting list. | Description matches block recovery and raise a warning for a person to confirm. |
| 14 | Late payment and disclosure penalties accrue monthly. Does a part month count as a whole month, and does a part payment reduce the balance from the next month? | Yes to both, which is the cautious reading. It reproduces the published worked examples. |
| 15 | Is a voluntary disclosure still due within 20 business days of discovery? Sources disagree. Which days count, given public holidays are not modelled? | The pack holds 20, counts Monday to Friday, and flags a late disclosure without blocking it. |
| 16 | Refunds: is the limit 5 years from the end of the period, and can a claim past it still be made before 1 January 2027? | Both are applied as stated, and a claim using the window is marked as transitional. Sources describe the window through different amending laws. |
| 17 | Apportionment under Cabinet Decision 149 of 2026: (a) sources say both "first tax year starting 1 October 2027" and "commencing after" it, so which? (b) Is rounding to a whole percent right for both methods? (c) What exactly follows an annual difference over AED 250,000? (d) Do standard and zero-rated supplies together count as those permitting recovery? | (a) From 1 October 2027 inclusive. (b) Yes. (c) A warning, with no further calculation. (d) Yes. Lines count as direct when given a recovery rate of 100 or 0. |
| 18 | FTA Audit File: the layout comes from the FTA's Requirements Document for Tax Accounting Software (October 2017), Appendix 5. Is there anything newer? That document defines no section markers, and Microsoft now describes a TXT format. Which code suits out-of-scope lines, given the document lists none? | Markers `CompInfoStart` and similar are on, from how other software's files are understood to look. Dates are DD-MM-YYYY, out of scope uses `OS`, and amendments use `OA` and `IA`. All of it is in the rule pack. |
| 19 | Bad debt relief: is the six months measured to the end of the period claiming it? Is it right to post to the adjustment column of the original emirate's box? Does a later recovery pay back in proportion? | Yes to all three. The FTA's software document supports the adjustment column. |
| 20 | Capital assets: does the first interval run from first use? On disposal, is each remaining interval treated as 100% or 0% taxable and settled together? Cabinet Decision 149 changed "single item of expenditure" to "business asset with a cost": does that change anything here? | Yes, yes, and no. |
| 21 | Profit margin scheme: does box 1 hold the margin net of VAT? Is the list of goods right (second-hand, antiques, collectors' items)? Acquisition costs reduce the margin here, following Article 29 as amended: is the condition about non-recoverable input tax one a person must check? | Yes, yes, and yes: nothing checks that condition, and the schedule shows what was entered. |
| 22 | Zero-rated exports: T07 warns when evidence is incomplete, and T08 blocks the earliest open period once 90 days have passed with no export date. Is blocking right? Only goods are checked: do exported services need a check too? | As described. |
| 23 | Cabinet Decision 149 of 2026 also restricts recovery on cash payments above a threshold still to be set, changes employee accommodation and benefits, and redefines composite supplies. How should these be modelled? | Not modelled. The blocked input list is unchanged. |
| 24 | Registration watch: are AED 375,000 and AED 187,500 right, and is the voluntary test on the same figure? Zero-rated supplies are entered with taxable supplies: is that right? | Yes, on all of these. |
| 25 | Does an incorrect return penalty of AED 500 apply on top of the disclosure penalty? Does a correction in the next return (up to AED 10,000) carry no penalty at all? | Yes to both. |
| 26 | Client approval by e-signature is a typed name, consent, and the signed-in identity, bound to the exact figures. Is that enough under the Electronic Transactions and Trust Services law for this purpose, or is a trust service needed? | Treated as enough, and it is a setting per client. |
| 27 | Peppol check: the TIN is the first ten digits of a TRN, but a Tax Group TRN's digits are not a member's TIN, so a group supplier can look unregistered. Is a warning the right level? | The lookup is off until switched on, and R08 stays a warning. |
| 28 | Which source counts as the Central Bank rate for the FX feed? No public API was found. | The feed reads whatever URL is configured, and is off until one is set. Rates can always be uploaded. |
